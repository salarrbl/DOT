# The 9 Iron Rules

1. **No measurement for hunters.** Don't compare to public bounty tables. Results show months later. Focus on process.
2. **Don't underestimate yourself.** You have a unique view. A novel test case will be gone tomorrow — share nothing repeatable.
3. **Hackers make tools. Tools don't make hackers.** Cexss is leverage, not identity.
4. **Every system can be weakened.** Human error never ends.
5. **Out-of-box thinking.** Ask "how is this *not* supposed to work?" not "does this work?"
6. **Chance is partly controllable.** More effort, more chances. Place of birth (geo, time, language) is uncontrollable.
7. **The target tells you what to do.** Don't overspray. Read the target.
8. **No rabbit hole.** If you can't validate an exploitation path in 2 hours, abandon it.
9. **Find your own path.** Manual or automation, hunter or builder — test both, then commit.
