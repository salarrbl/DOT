# Mental Models

Always loaded during every hunt.

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Abstraction** | Understand the app at high level first. Trash irrelevant details. Map flow before requests. |
| **Correspondence** | A small flaw in one part can give access to another. Think in chains, not isolated findings. |
| **Occam's Razor** | Simplest explanation is most probable. If you can't break a feature with 3 requests, your hypothesis is wrong — not your tooling. |
| **Pareto 80/20** | 80% of bugs come from 20% of misconfigurations. Deep-dive on auth, RBAC, password reset, registration, billing. |
| **Process > Outcome** | No fair measurement for hunters. Track: coverage gained, hypotheses tested, focused hours. Not keyboard time. |
