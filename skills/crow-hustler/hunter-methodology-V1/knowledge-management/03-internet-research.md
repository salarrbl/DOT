The rule:

Trigger

If:

Hunt becomes stuck
Recon becomes repetitive
No promising attack surface remains
Same payloads fail repeatedly
No findings after long exploration

Stop hunting.

Start learning.

Output

Every research session must produce at least one of:

new checklist
new payload
new methodology
new note
new hunting idea
a teach-back for the human (REQUIRED — see `agent/07-teach-back.md`)

Never consume information without producing something.

Afterwards: Human must read the teach-back before the next hunt.
Research that only the agent knows is wasted research.
